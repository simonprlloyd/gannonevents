The Story

More Than Just Music

A lifelong pianist who discovered DJing in 2000, Grant Gannon has built a reputation as Miami and Chicago's most trusted event music specialist. What started with small gatherings grew into a full-time career performing at weddings, corporate galas, schools and non-profits.

Music isn't just background noise — it's the pulse of your event. Grant's experience as a pianist, DJ and MC means he understands what it takes to read a room and keep every moment exactly where it needs to be.
